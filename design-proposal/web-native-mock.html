What changed

Needs you Working Done Idle Failed
  1. Needs you first. The amber sideways strip becomes a "Needs you 3" section on top. It now includes the blocked terminal agent ("tests"), which today shows only as a pink dot inside its workspace.
  2. One count. The header count, the toolbar's Board button, the app badge and the page title ("(3) Sheprd") all show agents waiting. Today the badge adds up questions and the toolbar shows a dot with no number.
  3. One color. Orange means needs you and nothing else. Working, done, idle and failed are grey and told apart by shape: ellipsis, check, ring, X. Today needs you is amber and pink on the same row, and working, done and idle are yellow, teal and green discs.
  4. Failed is visible (needs a failed state from the daemon). "Migrate config loader" exited with an error. Today the Home mark has no failed shape (it only knows idle, working, blocked, done and unknown), so it shows the last status reported, idle here; proposed it shows an X, in grey, not red.
  5. Wait time (needs waiting_since from the daemon for chats; terminal agents can use changed_ms). Needs-you rows say "waiting 2m". After 5 minutes the label gets a clock, semibold and orange.
  6. Monochrome logos. Every agent tile is grey with the logo in the text color. No orange Claude tile next to a status mark.
  7. PR as a word. "#212 Ready" and "#87 Behind" in grey, instead of a green or orange number.
  8. Access. rem sizes; question titles wrap to as many lines as they need instead of clipping at large text; a 2px focus ring on every link and button (shown statically on the settings button, Tab shows it anywhere); labels on icon-only buttons; 44px targets on touch. The toolbar stays glass, solid under reduced transparency and increased contrast.
  9. Motion. When an agent starts needing you, its row moves once into the Needs you section with a short spring, and the app badge and title count update at the same moment. Under reduced motion a cross-fade replaces the move. Nothing animates on refresh or as the age ticks. The working dot's blink and spin are removed, not only stopped under reduced motion (the app already stops them under reduced motion today).

Not changed: neutral greys, accent, font stack, layout, toolbar position, manifest colors.
