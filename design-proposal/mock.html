Unofficial redesign proposal, not made by the Sheprd team. Sample data is illustrative.

One ticket for every agent that needs you

A proposal to give Sheprd one visual system across the iPhone app, widgets, Watch, the web app and sheprd.org. Each agent that is waiting on you gets a ticket on a rail. The ticket shows the agent, the machine, the workspace and how long it has waited. You answer on the ticket, and it leaves the rail. Status colors, radius and type are the same everywhere.

iOS Home

Before: a plain List with "Needs you" cards on .background.secondary r22 and a red stroke (Views/Parts.swift:228-269), status as raw .red/.yellow/.teal/.green (Parts.swift:70-77), system blue tint (no AccentColor, ios/project.yml). After: waiting agents sit on a horizontal rail you can swipe, answered in place. Working agents are stubs grouped by machine. Done collapses into one stack. Heat orange is the app tint and marks only "Needs you". Ages are illustrative: they need a waiting_since timestamp from the remote control server.

Still frame: an answered ticket leaves the rail

Frame at 180 ms after Allow once: the ticket lifts and tilts, the next ticket slides left to close the gap. Success haptic fires on tap.

Motion: answering tears the ticket off the rail

The ticket lifts and slides off the rail in 300 ms, ease-out, with a success haptic. With Reduce Motion on, it fades out in place.
