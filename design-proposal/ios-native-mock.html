What changed

  1. Type: iOS text styles for all content: Large Title 34 bold for "Home", headers 17 semibold, row and card titles 17 (Body, Headline), meta line 15 (Subheadline), age 13 (Footnote). No 14 or 16. Tab labels are the system tab bar's own fixed 10 pt, not a text style the app sets. Content grows with the text size; at accessibility sizes titles and meta wrap up to 3 lines and the answer buttons stack.
  2. Order and count: Needs you, Working, Idle, Done. Only "Needs you" has a count; it equals the rows in that section, and the system tab badge is changed to show the same number.
  3. Orange marks needs you only: the waiting glyph, the age label after 5 minutes with its clock symbol, and the Needs you count. Card border and allow-once button are no longer red, and the offline icon is no longer orange.
  4. Age has a non-color cue: it always says "waiting 1m" in the label color; after 5 minutes it adds a clock symbol, turns semibold and takes the attention color. VoiceOver reads "Waiting for you, 7 minutes".
  5. Glyph shapes differ per state, so color is not needed to tell them apart. Only the waiting glyph is colored; working, done and failed use the secondary label color:
  1. Agent logos are monochrome: label-color mark on a system fill (`.fill.secondary`).
  2. Glass only on the nav bar buttons and the tab bar. The offline banner is the tab bar's bottom accessory (`tabViewBottomAccessory`), so it is part of the tab bar chrome, and the list's bottom inset clears it at every text size. Rows, headers, answer cards and their buttons are opaque. Under Reduce Transparency the chrome turns solid.
  3. Targets are at least 44 pt: nav buttons, rows, answer buttons, tabs. Answer buttons are `.bordered` in the label color with allow-once `.borderedProminent` in the accent, and stack when they do not fit (`ViewThatFits`).
  4. Colors are system label, fill and grouped background colors in light and dark, with Increase Contrast values. The attention color is a Color Set so 13 pt text passes 4.5:1 (5.3:1 light, 8.3:1 dark). Open question: the meta line uses the system secondary label color, about 3.4:1 on white in light, so "system label colors" and "4.5:1 for text" disagree there; the owner decides. The tab bar's selected label and badge are drawn by the system and keep its colors.
  5. Motion: when an agent starts needing you, its row moves once from its place in Working to Needs you with a short spring (a cross-fade under Reduce Motion) and one notification haptic fires on that frame. Nothing moves on refresh, a row update or an age tick. The waiting glyph does not pulse, and the working glyph's effect stops under Reduce Motion.
