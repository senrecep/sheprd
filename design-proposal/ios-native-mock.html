What changed

  1. Type: iOS text styles only. Large Title 34 bold for "Home", headers 17 semibold, row and card titles 17 (Body, Headline), meta line 15 (Subheadline), age 13 (Footnote), tab labels 10. No 14 or 16.
  2. Order and count: Needs you, Working, Idle, Done. Only "Needs you" has a count; it equals its rows and the system tab badge.
  3. Orange appears three times only: the waiting glyph, the age label after 5 minutes and the Needs you count. Card border, allow-once button and offline icon are no longer orange.
  4. Age has a non-color cue: it always says "waiting 1m"; after 5 minutes it adds a clock symbol and turns semibold.
  5. Glyph shapes differ per state, not only their color:
  1. Agent logos are monochrome: label-color mark on a system fill (`.fill.secondary`).
  2. Glass only on the nav bar buttons, tab bar and the floating banner capsule, which is system-style chrome over the list, not a list row. Rows, headers, answer cards and their buttons are opaque. Under Reduce Transparency the chrome turns solid.
  3. Targets are at least 44 pt: nav buttons, rows, answer buttons, tabs.
  4. Colors are system label, fill and grouped background colors in light and dark, with Increase Contrast values. The attention color is a Color Set so 13 pt text passes 4.5:1 (5.3:1 light, 8.3:1 dark).
  5. Motion: when an agent starts needing you, its row fades and slides in once (cross-fade only under Reduce Motion) and one notification haptic fires on that frame. Nothing moves on refresh or on an age tick, and nothing pulses.
